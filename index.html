HOW TO PLAY

You are hiding inside Weddington Middle School after hours. Survive from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM, five nights in a row.

LEFT & RIGHT DOORS

Your security room has two entrances. Press LEFT DOOR or RIGHT DOOR to slam the heavy shutter shut. A closed door cannot be entered — but it drains power fast, so don’t leave them closed forever.

SECURITY CAMERAS

Press CAMERAS to open the monitor and check the ten feeds around the school. It’s the only way to track a teacher’s route before they reach your door — but you can’t see your own doors while the monitor is open, so listen carefully.

THE TEACHERS

Five teachers patrol the halls. Each moves differently — learn their patterns. When one reaches a doorway, a warning light above that door will flash and you’ll hear them. Close that door before time runs out, or they will get in.

FLASHLIGHT

Hold FLASHLIGHT to brighten the room and clearly illuminate whoever is standing in your doorways. It drains power while held.

POWER

Everything you use drains the school’s backup power — watch the USAGE meter. If power hits 0%, both doors force open, the lights die, and you become extremely vulnerable.

SURVIVE

Reach 6:00 AM without being caught. Good luck.
