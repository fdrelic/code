HOW TO PLAY

You are hiding inside Weddington Middle School after hours. You must survive from 12:00 AM until 6:00 AM for five nights.

SECURITY CAMERAS

Press CAMERAS to open the monitor and check the ten camera feeds around the school. Watching the cameras is the only way to track where the teachers are.

THE TEACHERS

Five teachers patrol the halls looking for students. Each one moves differently — learn their patterns. If a teacher reaches the door outside the security room, you must catch them with your flashlight in time or they will get in.

FLASHLIGHT

Hold FLASHLIGHT to light up the doorway. If someone is standing there, keep the light on them to scare them off. It also drains power.

POWER

Everything you use drains the school’s backup power. If it runs out, the lights die and you become far more vulnerable. Use your systems wisely.

SURVIVE

Reach 6:00 AM without being caught. Good luck.
